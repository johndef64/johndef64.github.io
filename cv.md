### Doctoral thesis

*From Trans-Omic Networks to Knowledge Graphs: Encoding and Exploiting Biological
Semantics for Multi-Omics Data Integration with Graph Neural Networks.* A reusable
ontology-grounded substrate coupling biomedical knowledge with per-sample
multi-omic measurements, and heterogeneous graph neural networks that learn over
it for relation discovery and molecular subtype classification.
