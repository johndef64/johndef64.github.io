## For students

I supervise theses and internships on multi-omics integration, biomedical
knowledge graphs and graph neural networks, usually on TCGA cohorts. Typical work
involves building or curating a knowledge graph that links genes, proteins,
pathways and known interactions with provenance tracking, then training graph
models on it for subtype classification or link prediction. Write to me with a
short note on your background and what interests you.
